# Accounts and saved data

## Quick or saved game?

A **quick game** is ephemeral, can be anonymous, and is not attached to a DataSpace.
Choose it even while signed in when you do not want history or settings persisted.

A **saved game** uses the current DataSpace, allowing groups, preferred profile,
intensity, and history to remain together over time.

## DataSpaces and groups

A DataSpace is a separate ownership area inside an account, such as “Friends” or
“Couple night”. Groups store names for recurring sessions. Select the correct DataSpace
and group before creating a persisted Room; IDs from another DataSpace do not bypass
ownership checks.

## Sessions and security

The Account screen lists active account sessions and can revoke another device. Use a
unique password. Activation and reset links are one-time secrets and should never be
forwarded.

## Export and deletion

Export downloads account-owned data as JSON; handle it as private information.
**Delete account** permanently removes the account and saved game data after username
confirmation. Ephemeral Rooms are not included in later exports.
